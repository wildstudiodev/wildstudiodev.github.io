Delete your MyCoachOS account

In the app (fastest)

• Coaches: open the menu (☰), then Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

• Parents and adult players: on your home screen tap ⋮, then Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Cloud deletion happens straight away when you are signed in. Clearing an offline coach phone deletes data on that phone only; log in or email us to delete an existing cloud account.

Can't get into the app?

Email wildstudiodev@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my MyCoachOS account". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What's deleted

• Coach accounts: your login; your players, sessions, availability, messages and development notes on our servers; and all MyCoachOS data on the phone you delete from. Parents lose access to your sessions.

• Parent and player accounts: your login, your details, and your link to your coach.

What's kept, and why

• Billing records (platform fee records and invoices) are kept for 6 years under the draft accounting-retention policy (owner/accountant confirmation pending), with the coach's name and email removed.

• When a parent or player deletes their account, the coach keeps their own records of past sessions. Messages you sent stay in the coach's history but are no longer linked to your account.
