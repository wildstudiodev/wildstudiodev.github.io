MyCoachOS privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

MyCoachOS is an app that helps independent football coaches run their coaching: weekly sessions, bookings, rearranging, payments and optional player development notes. It's made by Noble Services Ltd (company number 14718454, registered office 28 Glynwood Park, Farnworth, Bolton, England, BL4 7SX), trading as Wild Studio Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: wildstudiodev@gmail.com. No ICO registration reference has been provided; registration remains an owner release action.

Who is responsible for which data

• Coaches' players and families: the coach decides what to record about their players and is the data controller for that information. We store and process it on the coach's behalf as their data processor.

• Your own account (coach, parent or adult player login, and how you use the app): we are the data controller.

If you're a parent or player and want to know what a coach holds about you, ask the coach first. You can also contact us and we'll help.

What we collect

Who · What · Why

Coaches · Name, email, business name, invite code, schedule settings; if you take card payments, your Stripe account ID · To run your account and your diary

Players (entered by the coach) · Name, date of birth, contact details for a parent or guardian, notes the coach adds, sessions, attendance, payments, optional development notes · So the coach can run sessions. Date of birth is used to keep messaging with under-18s going through a parent or guardian.

Parents and adult players · Name, email, which player you're linked to, messages you send, booking and rescheduling actions · So you can see and manage sessions with your coach

Everyone · Basic technical data (app version, error logs) · To keep the app working

We don't sell data, show adverts, or use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Under-18s

Players under 18 never have a one-to-one message thread with the coach. Messages go through their parent or guardian, and under-18s can't create their own account. If a date of birth isn't known, we treat the player as under 18.

Where it's stored

• The coach's phone: MyCoachOS keeps the coach's diary on their phone so it works pitch-side without signal.

• Our cloud: if the coach turns on cloud sync, data is stored with Supabase in the London (UK) region.

• Card payments: these go through Stripe (Stripe Connect Standard). Money goes straight to the coach's own Stripe account; we never hold session payments. Stripe is a separate controller for the payment data it processes.

• The app itself is distributed through Google Play.

Lawful bases

• Contract: to provide your account and the service.

• Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and working, and safeguarding.

• Legal obligation: keeping billing records.

• For player data, the coach is responsible for having a lawful basis (usually contract or legitimate interests), and for telling families about it.

How long we keep it

• Account and coaching data is kept until the account is deleted.

• When a coach deletes their account, their players, diary, messages and development notes are deleted from our servers straight away.

• Billing records (platform fee records and invoices) are retained for accounting obligations; the draft policy is 6 years and needs accountant/owner confirmation, with the coach's name and email removed.

• Provider backup/export expiry is still being verified. We do not promise a seven-day expiry without evidence.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete or restrict it, or to object to how we use it. Email wildstudiodev@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You can delete your own account in the app (see Delete your account). You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) at any time.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above when things change, and tell coaches in the app about significant changes.
